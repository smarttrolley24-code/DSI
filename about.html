About DSI Hospital

Where wellness begins — compassionate, expert medical care in the heart of Gulistan-e-Jauhar.

Hospital Profile

A private hospital built around our community

DSI Hospital is a private hospital established in 2023 at A-1, Block 4, Gulistan-e-Jauhar, Karachi. We provide compassionate, expert medical care for all your health needs — from 24/7 emergency services to specialized treatments, supported by modern facilities and a team of dedicated doctors.

Our range of care includes women's health (gynae & obs), IVF & fertility, NICU, pediatrics, cardiology, ENT, urology, internal medicine and aesthetic care — with a 24-hour pharmacy, laboratory and state-of-the-art operation theaters on site.
