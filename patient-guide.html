Before Your Visit

A smoother hospital visit starts with clear information

1. Confirm your appointment

Call the hospital or submit an appointment request. Confirm the doctor and time before travelling.

2. Bring relevant records

Bring identification plus any prescriptions, test reports or previous medical records relevant to your consultation.

3. Arrive early

Allow time for registration and locating the appropriate clinic or service counter.

4. Ask questions

Write down important questions and follow the clinician’s advice for tests, medication or follow-up.

Important

Hospital policies, payment methods, lab preparation instructions and visiting hours should be confirmed directly with DSI Hospital before this website goes live.
