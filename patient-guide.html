Patient Guide

Everything you need for a smooth visit to DSI Hospital.

Before Your Visit

A smoother hospital visit starts with clear information

1. Book your appointment

Call 0314-0725738 (WhatsApp), 021-34016615, or use our online appointment form. General OPD runs daily from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM; specialist timings are confirmed when you book.

2. Bring relevant records

Bring your CNIC or ID plus any prescriptions, test reports or previous medical records relevant to your consultation.

3. Arrive early

Allow time for registration at reception before your consultation. Our on-site pharmacy and laboratory mean fewer trips elsewhere.

4. Ask questions

Write down important questions and follow your clinician's advice for tests, medication or follow-up visits.

Good to know

Emergency: Open 24 hours, including a dedicated Gynae & Obs emergency.

Pharmacy & Laboratory: Open 24 hours inside the hospital.

OPD: General OPD by RMO, daily 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM.

Ultrasound & lab preparation: Some tests require fasting — confirm when booking.
